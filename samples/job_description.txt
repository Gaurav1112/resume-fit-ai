Staff Backend Engineer — Platform
Helios Financial Technologies | Remote (US timezones) | Full-time

About the role
Helios builds real-time treasury infrastructure for mid-market banks. You will
join the Platform team, which owns the event backbone, the service mesh, and the
deployment tooling that every product team depends on. This is a hands-on staff
role: you will design systems, write code, and set technical direction across
teams.

What you'll do
- Design and operate event-driven systems handling millions of financial
  transactions per day
- Lead the decomposition of remaining monolithic services into well-bounded
  microservices
- Own the reliability of the platform, including on-call, SLOs, and incident
  review
- Set standards for API design, testing, and observability across the engineering org
- Mentor senior engineers and act as a technical reviewer for major designs

Requirements
- 8+ years of professional backend software engineering experience
- Expert-level Java, including Spring Boot
- Deep experience with Apache Kafka and event-driven architecture
- Strong background in distributed systems and microservices at scale
- Production experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration
- Hands-on with AWS
- Strong SQL and relational data modeling (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform)
- Demonstrated technical leadership: mentoring, design review, cross-team influence
- Excellent written communication

Preferred
- Experience in fintech, payments, or banking
- Python for tooling and automation
- Familiarity with service mesh technologies (Istio or Linkerd)
- Experience with GraphQL
- Prior experience running a platform or infrastructure team

Nice to have
- Open-source contributions
- Public speaking or technical writing
- Experience with Rust

What we offer
Competitive compensation, equity, and a fully remote team across US timezones.
Applicants must be authorized to work in the United States.
